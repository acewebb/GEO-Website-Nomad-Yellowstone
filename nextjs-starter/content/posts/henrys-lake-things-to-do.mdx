## Henry's Lake: Island Park's Trophy Fishing Destination

Henry's Lake sits at 6,472 feet elevation in a stunning mountain valley just north of Island Park, Idaho — about 15 minutes from the Last Chance/Island Park corridor and 30 minutes from the West Yellowstone entrance to Yellowstone National Park. The lake is famous among anglers worldwide for its trophy-sized hybrid cutthroat-rainbow trout, with fish regularly exceeding 5 pounds and occasional catches surpassing 10 pounds.

### Fishing on Henry's Lake

Henry's Lake is a shallow, nutrient-rich lake that produces exceptionally large trout relative to its modest size (~6 miles long). The shallow depth means fish are concentrated and accessible even to intermediate anglers — but fooling them with the right presentation requires skill.

**Season and Regulations:**
- The lake opens for fishing in late May (exact date set annually by Idaho Fish and Game) and remains productive through September
- Brook trout season typically opens later — check current IDFG regulations
- Special regulations apply: barbless hooks, catch-and-release periods, and species-specific limits vary by year

**Best Fishing Methods:**
- **Float tubes:** The most popular and productive method. Float tubes let you access weed beds and drop-offs where trophy fish cruise. Early morning before wind builds is prime.
- **Small boats:** Aluminum boats and small inflatables work well. The lake has a public boat ramp on the north shore and a state park launch on the west shore.
- **Shore fishing:** Possible but significantly less productive. The best shoreline access is along the state park campground.

**Target Species:**
- Yellowstone cutthroat trout (native)
- Brook trout
- Cutthroat-rainbow hybrids — the lake's signature trophy fish, known for explosive fights and vibrant coloring

**Best Months:** June (post-opening adrenaline, hungry fish) and September (pre-winter feeding, fewer anglers, spectacular fall colors surrounding the lake).

### Camping Near Henry's Lake

The **Henry's Lake State Park campground** occupies a prime location on the lake's west shore with direct water access. Sites include electric hookups, a boat ramp, and a fish cleaning station. The campground typically fills on summer weekends — reservations through [Idaho State Parks](https://parksandrecreation.idaho.gov) are essential for July and August.

Additional camping options in the area:
- **Dispersed camping** on surrounding Caribou-Targhee National Forest land (free, no facilities)
- **Private campgrounds** along the US-20 corridor between Island Park and West Yellowstone
- **Cabin rentals** throughout the Island Park/Henry's Lake area — see our [Best Cabin Rentals Near Yellowstone](/intel/best-cabin-rentals-near-yellowstone) guide

### Beyond Fishing: Things to Do at Henry's Lake

**Wildlife viewing:** The Henry's Lake area supports moose, osprey, bald eagles, sandhill cranes, and white pelicans. The lake's shallow marshy edges are excellent birding habitat, especially in spring and fall migration.

**Photography:** The Centennial Mountains form the backdrop to the east, creating dramatic sunrise compositions reflected in calm morning water. Fall aspen color (late September) transforms the surrounding hillsides into gold.

**Scenic loop:** Henry's Lake is on the route between Island Park and the [Mesa Falls Scenic Byway](/intel/mesa-falls-scenic-byway-guide) — combine both in a full day trip with a morning ATV tour from Island Park.
