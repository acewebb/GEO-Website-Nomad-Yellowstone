## Mesa Falls Scenic Byway: Island Park's Best Drive

The Mesa Falls Scenic Byway is a 28-mile paved loop off US-20 near Ashton, Idaho — about 30 minutes south of Island Park. The byway passes through the Caribou-Targhee National Forest and features two powerful waterfalls on the Henry's Fork of the Snake River: Upper Mesa Falls (114 feet) and Lower Mesa Falls (65 feet).

**Upper Mesa Falls**

Upper Mesa Falls is the star attraction — a 114-foot cascade that ranks among the most impressive waterfalls in Idaho. A short paved trail (approximately 0.3 miles) leads from the parking area to a series of overlook platforms with direct views of the falls. The falls are most dramatic in June when snowmelt swells the Henry's Fork.

**Lower Mesa Falls**

Lower Mesa Falls drops 65 feet through a narrow basalt canyon. The overlook is accessible via a short trail from the Lower Mesa Falls pullout. The canyon framing makes this waterfall particularly photogenic.

**Driving the Byway**

- **Start**: Turn east off US-20 near Ashton onto Highway 47
- **Distance**: 28-mile loop returning to US-20
- **Time**: 1.5–2 hours with photo stops
- **Best light**: Early morning or late afternoon
- **Season**: Typically open late May through October (snow-dependent)

**Combining with ATV Tours**

The Mesa Falls Scenic Byway pairs perfectly with a morning guided ATV tour from Island Park. Do the backcountry in the morning with Nomad Yellowstone's passenger-only ATV tours, then drive the scenic byway in the afternoon. The two activities showcase completely different sides of the Island Park landscape — rugged alpine ridgelines versus dramatic volcanic river canyons.
